Kismet asset credits
====================

Attribution for art assets ported into this fork. The root CREDITS.md is
pokeemerald-expansion's contributor table and is not ours to edit; this file is
for the art we bring in.

Two upstream credit files feed this list, and they describe DIFFERENT sprites
that happen to share a name. Check which file an asset actually came from
before copying an attribution across:

  A. pokemonHnS/credits.txt          - the HnS fork's own sprite set
  B. Assets/FireRed Overworld Sprites/credits.txt
     (PokeCommunity "Fire Red Overworld Sprite Resource",
      https://www.pokecommunity.com/threads/fire-red-overworld-sprite-resource.407124/)

The Dragon Tamer is the worked example: source A lists one under its group
credit, source B credits Kalarie. Both are correct - they are separate sheets
(144x32 sharing the Lady palette vs 160x32 with its own). Kismet ships B's.

Hoenn overworld sprites
-----------------------

Gym Leaders   Brawly, Flannery, Liza, Norman, Roxanne, Tate, Wallace,
              Wattson, Winona
Elite Four    Drake, Glacia, Phoebe, Sidney          Poffin_Case
Frontier      Greta, Lucy, Spenser, Tasso

Wally                                 Poffin_Case (inferred - see below)

  Attribution from Moritz, who sourced the sheets: Poffin_Case made the Hoenn
  Gym Leader, Elite Four and Frontier Brain overworld sprites. Source A spells
  the name "PoffinCase"; treat the two as the same artist.

  ⚠ Wally is credited by inference, not by that statement. He arrived in the
  same batch from the same folder, but he is not a Gym Leader, Elite Four member
  or Frontier Brain, so the statement does not cover him. Confirm before relying
  on it.

  These sheets came from a loose Assets folder with no credits file, which is why
  this section previously read UNATTRIBUTED - both Crystal decomps' credits and
  both sprite-resource credits files had been searched without result.

  ⚠ A pixel comparison had argued AGAINST this credit and was wrong. Source A
  credits a Wallace OW sprite to RavePossum / PoffinCase; that sheet and the one
  shipped here share no colour mapping, and the difference was read as evidence
  the credit did not transfer. It only ever showed the two sheets are different
  versions - an artist making two versions of the same character defeats that
  test entirely. Direct testimony from whoever sourced an asset outranks
  inference from the asset itself.

  Phoebe and Roxanne were 17- and 18-colour images and had to be reduced to fit
  4bpp. The merges are recorded here for honesty:
    Phoebe   (72,48,48)     2 px -> (58,58,25)
    Roxanne  (255,255,255)  4 px -> (248,216,184)
             (72,48,48)     2 px -> (58,58,25)
  Six pixels total across 9,216 per sheet. Every other sheet converted with no
  colour loss.
